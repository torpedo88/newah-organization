## If you replace a photograph

Update this file **and** `lib/constants/photo-credits.ts` together. The site
renders from the constant; this file is the human-readable record of where each
image came from and under what terms.

Photographs taken by the chapter itself need no attribution here — and are
better than any of these. Replace them as they become available.
